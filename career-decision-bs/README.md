# Does parental career support buffer or amplify?

A simulation study prepared for *Behavioral Sciences* (MDPI).

The paper starts from a published finding it does not attempt to replicate.
Pan and He (2026, *Behavioral Sciences*) reported, for 407 female
undergraduates at four non-elite Chinese universities, that the association
between career anxiety and career decision-making difficulties was *stronger*
among students reporting more frequent parental career support. Within Social
Cognitive Career Theory that is the wrong sign. This project asks what would
have to be true of the process generating those variables over time for a
cross-section of it to look like that.

## What is and is not data

There is **one** empirical input: the six correlations Pan and He report for
their sample. They are transcribed in `analysis/calibrate.py` as `TARGET` and
used as a calibration objective. Everything else in the paper is produced by
running the model.

No student, response or observation in this repository is real. Every table
and figure is generated by `analysis/run_all.py` and `analysis/figures.py`
under a fixed seed (`meta.seed` in `tables/summary.json`); re-running them
reproduces the manuscript's numbers exactly. The manuscript text interpolates
those numbers from `tables/summary.json` rather than restating them, so the
prose cannot drift from what the code computed.

All 32 parameters are declared once in `analysis/params.py` with a value, an
interval and a provenance label. Appendix A of the manuscript is generated
from that registry. One parameter is bounded by a cited source (measurement
error, bounded by the composite reliabilities Pan and He report); the rest are
modelling choices, and 12 of them are fixed by calibration.

## The model

`analysis/model.py` runs a weekly loop over a 30-week decision horizon for a
cohort of simulated students. Each student carries unresolved uncertainty,
career exploration, decision-making self-efficacy, and career anxiety split
into a stable dispositional component and a component that responds to the
situation. The loop is the one social cognitive theory describes: anxiety
reduces both the amount of exploration and its informational yield,
exploration reduces uncertainty and supplies mastery experience, mastery
builds self-efficacy, and self-efficacy sustains exploration.

Parental involvement enters through three channels, whose relative shares are
what the analysis varies:

- **reassurance** — lowers anxiety without changing the informational situation;
- **scaffolding** — raises what the student's own exploring yields;
- **substitution** — resolves uncertainty on the student's behalf.

The three have the same sign in a cross-sectional correlation with difficulty
and quite different implications for self-efficacy, because only scaffolding
leaves the student a mastery experience to attribute to themselves. That is
the distinction the paper argues instruments measuring *frequency* cannot make.

## What the analysis does

1. **Calibrate** (`calibrate.py`) — coordinate descent over a free subset of
   parameters until a simulated cross-section reproduces the six reported
   correlations. Final RMSE 0.113; five of six within 0.07. The substitution
   share is deliberately *excluded* from the free set: it is the quantity the
   study is about, and calibrating it would assume the answer.
2. **Reproduce the original analysis** (`estimators.py`) — the same
   conditional process model, mediation plus a moderated direct path, with
   percentile bootstrap intervals, implemented from first principles. Its
   self-test recovers known coefficients from synthetic data.
3. **Vary the kind of involvement** — sweep the directive share from 0 to 1.
4. **Search the space** — a factorial grid over the five parameters governing
   conflict and involvement, recording where amplification arises.
5. **Check precision** — repeated simulated studies at n = 407.
6. **Check identification** — which correlations each parameter actually moves.

## What it found

The expected explanation is wrong. Directive involvement does not amplify; it
buffers most strongly of all, because it resolves uncertainty whether or not
the student is in any condition to act. The interaction runs from +0.017 at a
wholly scaffolding involvement to −0.370 at a wholly directive one.

Amplification arises instead from **scaffolding**, and it arises for an
uncomfortable reason: scaffolding multiplies a capacity anxiety has already
suppressed, so it reaches the students who least need it. Moving from low to
high involvement reduces simulated difficulty by 0.167 for the least anxious
quartile and by 0.004 for the most anxious.

Across 243 parameter combinations amplification appears in 14 (5.8%), and
never where the directive share reaches one half. At n = 407 the simulated
interaction has mean −0.184 (SD 0.041) and is detected 99.7% of the time, so
sampling noise is not a sufficient account of the discrepancy either.

## Layout

```
analysis/    params.py  model.py  estimators.py  calibrate.py
             run_all.py  figures.py  calibrated.json
tables/      t01..t08 TSVs, t0A_params.tsv, summary.json, traj_by_trait.npy
figures/     figure1_model.png  figure2_trajectories.png  figure3_slopes.png
build/       content.py  tables_spec.py  refs.py  bibliography.py
             build_docx.py  build_cover_letter.py  behavscitemplate.dot
```

## Reproducing

```
python3 analysis/run_all.py     # ~30 s; writes tables/ and summary.json
python3 analysis/figures.py     # writes figures/
python3 build/build_docx.py     # writes the manuscript .docx
python3 build/build_cover_letter.py
```

`build_docx.py` repackages the MDPI `.dot` template as a `.docx`, applies the
journal's own paragraph styles, and resolves `[[key]]` markers in `content.py`
into APA author-date citations with an alphabetical reference list. A key
suffixed `_n` renders narratively, as Author (Year).

## Figures

Figures carry no explanatory notes. Whatever a note would say is stated in the
body text instead, so the reader meets it in the argument rather than in small
type beneath a picture. Tables keep their notes.

## References

`build/bibliography.py` holds 34 entries. Each was kept only where its author
list was seen in a retrieved record rather than recalled, and fields that
could not be confirmed — volume, pages — are omitted rather than invented.
